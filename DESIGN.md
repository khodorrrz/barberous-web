# Design System: Barberous

## Overview

Preserve the minimal luxury editorial identity. The client replaced haircut imagery with a logo-led hero and a service menu. Keep the actual logo and owner portrait; no haircut gallery.

## Colors

Near-black #0b0b0b is the main ground. Client-supplied charcoal #181818 appears in the hero brand panel and alternating menu categories. The team section uses #121212. Off-white #f5f5f2 carries text and the closing contact panel. Muted #ababaa text and #343434 rules support hierarchy.

## Typography

Self-hosted Manrope for English and Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic. The English headline pairs a heavy first line with a lighter, end-aligned second line. Category titles use 26-38px type; menu labels are 16px desktop and 14px mobile. USD prices stay LTR with tabular numerals.

## Layout

A 1180px shell holds navigation, the split editorial hero and contact information. Four full-width menu categories alternate charcoal and near-black. On desktop the category heading sits beside its service list. Each row aligns name, USD price and arrow. At 760px and below titles stack above rows. The grooming package alone has a contents description.

The team section pairs biographies and the owner's 4:5 portrait. The off-white closing panel contains a dark booking button. Arabic uses RTL; prices, telephone and Instagram handle stay LTR. No fixed mobile bar or reserved bottom space.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat tonal sections and fine rules; no decorative shadows or gradients.

## Shapes

Square panels, rectangular buttons and crisp dividers. Preserve the supplied logo.

## Components

Service rows open localized WhatsApp requests at the exact client-supplied prices. Sending and confirmation remain manual. Navigation contains Services, The barbers and Visit us. Instagram appears in the hero and contact details.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Preserve twelve services, four categories, all supplied USD prices and package contents.
- Keep charcoal sectioning subtle and monochrome.
- Do not reintroduce haircut imagery, gallery links or the fixed mobile booking bar.
- Do not invent reviews or change prices without client instruction.
